# Changelog

All notable changes to The Story Behind, formerly London in Dance, are documented here.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- Added a Stories gallery of six performances, grouped by year, with performance photography and titles revealed on hover or keyboard focus for linked stories.
- Published the English-language _Auguries of Innocence_ essay, with performance photography, the venue and viewing date, and production credits.
- Added an About page with the journal's purpose, an editor bio, and links for collaborations and other enquiries.

### Changed

- Rebranded London in Dance as The Story Behind, an independent art journal, with a responsive magazine cover and shared navigation across Home, What's On, Stories, and About.
- Moved performance browsing to What's On. Redesigned the list with a centred grid, 4:5 posters, and cards that open the official event link.
- Replaced weekly and monthly date filters with Now on, Ending soon, and Coming up. The seven-day Ending soon window uses London calendar dates. Running performances are sorted by closing date and upcoming performances by opening date.
- Added poster labels for days remaining or opening dates, with terracotta for running performances. Full performance dates remain below the posters.
- Refreshed listings from London Coliseum, Sadler's Wells, and Royal Ballet and Opera through 17 November 2026.

### Fixed

- Updated the browser tab title to The Story Behind.
- Aligned navigation across the cover and interior pages.
- Enabled direct visits and refreshes on Vercel for client-side routes, including Stories and individual essays.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-06-22

### Added

- New genre categories: Jazz, National Dance
- New performance listings across all venues

### Changed

- Reclassified flamenco, tango, and kathak performances from
  Contemporary to National Dance
- Updated Ballet Icons Gala 2027 title and ticket link

## [1.0.0] - 2026-06-15

### Added

- Initial launch of London in Dance
- Ballet and Contemporary performance listings from London Coliseum,
  Sadler's Wells, and Royal Opera House
- Genre and date filters
- Performance detail modal with booking link
